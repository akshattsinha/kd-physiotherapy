Recovery Journey

Our 5-Step Recovery Process

We guide you transparently through every stage of therapy, from diagnostic consults to complete recovery.

1
Step 1

Consultation & Assessment

We evaluate your clinical history, inspect joints/muscles, test range of motion, and assess baseline pain levels.

2
Step 2

Diagnosis & Treatment Planning

Dr. KD creates a custom therapy schedule, detailing session counts, equipment needs, and home exercise lists.

3
Step 3

Physiotherapy Sessions

Experience pain-free treatments using manual therapy, electrotherapy (TENS/Ultrasound), and target exercises.

4
Step 4

Progress Monitoring

We track improvements weekly, adjust movement loads, and tweak modalities to speed up recovery.

5
Step 5

Long-Term Recovery Support

Get maintenance programs, posture check instructions, and direct therapist contact for questions.
